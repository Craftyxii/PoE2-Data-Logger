This is an upstream export label, not a claim about the installed game version.

Canonical names, unique-stash row IDs, visual identities and inventory
footprints come from data/uniques.json. Base-name artwork alternatives come
from data/base_items.json. Original PNG artwork comes from the same pinned
export's data/Art tree. The manifest records immutable URLs and SHA-256 hashes.
PNG bytes, aspect ratios and alpha channels are retained without rendering or
resizing. Repeated files are deduplicated by their exact SHA-256 digest.

The unique export does not provide an authoritative relationship to base-item
Metadata IDs. base_id is therefore null; shared-art base references are visual
alternatives and must not be treated as a unique-to-base identity mapping.
The export's data/uniques.html explicitly states that game files cannot confirm
actual unique availability or drop rates. This catalog does not claim that all
listed items are obtainable, current, or eligible for Ritual rewards.

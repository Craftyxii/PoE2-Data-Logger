Path of Exile 2 Atlas data and artwork

Path of Exile and Path of Exile 2 are trademarks of Grinding Gear Games.
The exported game data and artwork remain copyright Grinding Gear Games.
PoE2 Data Logger is not affiliated with or endorsed by Grinding Gear Games.

Atlas layout, passive effects and stat descriptions come from the RePoE PoE2
export (https://github.com/repoe-fork/poe2). Choice options and their numeric
stats come from the RePoE raw PoE2 passive tables
(https://github.com/repoe-fork/dat-export).

The bundled node icons, backgrounds and passive frames are game artwork
distributed with Sweet Vision's PoE2 build converter
(https://github.com/PraedythXIV/poe2-build-converter). Its original source-code
license and third-party notices are included in UPSTREAM-LICENSE.txt and
UPSTREAM-NOTICES.md. These upstream documents describe that project's code
and dependencies; those dependencies are not added to PoE2 Data Logger.

SOURCES.md records the pinned source revisions, export dates, rebuild steps
and artwork limitations. art-provenance.json records the artwork sources and
hashes. The exported internal game build is 4.5.5.2; this is not a claim about
the user's installed public game patch.

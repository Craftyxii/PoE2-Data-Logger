Rite of the Nameless Modifier Catalog

Verified: 19 October 2026.

Sources:
- PoE2DB, https://poe2db.tw/us/Rite_of_the_Nameless
  The complete published "Foretold Proliferation Mods /73" table supplies the
  73 source rows: 60 bounty variants, six proliferation effects, six local
  effects, and one untranslated very-rare-unique flag.
- vaal.tools, https://www.vaal.tools/mechanics/foretold_proliferation/
  Corroborates the six proliferation templates and their scope. The site
  reported PoE2 v0.5.5d when verified.
- PoE 2 Ref, https://poe2ref.com/mechanics/ritual
  Its Rite of the Nameless modifier pool corroborates the 60 bounty variants
  and six proliferation templates. Its probabilities are not bundled here.

This catalog records factual game modifier data and source attribution. It does
not reproduce guide prose, site assets, or artwork. Original modifier wording is
retained in each row's provenance; display wording only normalizes singular
grammar. Three untranslated raw stat labels remain explicitly untranslated.

The catalog is an offline snapshot of published membership. It does not establish
live league eligibility, probabilities, numerical stacking formulas, or a monster
density estimate. The rarity flag is not assigned an invented unique-item name.

Identifiers beginning "rite:", "custom:rite:", and "logger:rite:" belong to this
application. They are not official or datamined game IDs. Content-derived IDs
remain stable while the corresponding source wording is unchanged. Typed custom
entries are explicitly unverified user data and cannot change bundled defaults.

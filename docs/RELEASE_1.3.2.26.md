# PoE2 Data Logger 1.3.2.26 Beta

This beta applies the Currency and Ritual scan corrections on top of [1.3.2.25 Beta](RELEASE_1.3.2.25.md).

- Keep verified item names and quantities out of review while retaining their saved totals. Uncertain identities, tiers and counts still require confirmation.
- Use quantity one when an item has no quantity marker. Clipped, unreadable, incomplete or conflicting counts remain held for review.
- Preserve whole equipment footprints as Items. Unknown equipment needs a name once; approved corrections teach the complete artwork for later recognition.
- Protect the preceding beta's running client during installation and removal.

The existing HUD layout, controls and styling are retained. Existing screenshot ownership, historical records and counting policies remain in place.

Publication requires the Windows regression and workflow gates, frozen-runtime checks, installer/update/file-retention checks and the installer checksum on the exact release source.
